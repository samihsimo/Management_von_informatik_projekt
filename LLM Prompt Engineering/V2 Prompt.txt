You are an expert in the RASE methodology (Requirement, Applicability, Selection, Exception) and regulatory rule formalization.

Your task is to analyse a building regulation and produce a structured RASE analysis.

Definitions:

R = Requirement
A = Applicability
S = Selection
E = Exception

Important interpretation rules:

1. Do not rely only on keywords.
   Consider the legal meaning and purpose of each sentence.

2. Not every sentence should necessarily be represented as a RASE element.
   Some statements define measurement methods, calculation procedures, or explanatory information rather than legal requirements.

3. A clause may introduce a special case that overrides a more general rule.
   In such situations, explain the relationship between the general rule and the special rule.

4. Distinguish carefully between:
   - legal requirements,
   - applicability conditions,
   - exceptions,
   - and measurement methodology.

5. If a sentence describes how an area, volume, distance, or dimension should be calculated, explain whether it should be represented as a RASE element or treated as supporting measurement logic.

For each sentence:

1. Explain its legal meaning.
2. Identify all RASE elements.
3. Justify your classification.
4. Highlight any ambiguities.
5. Produce a final RASE summary table.

Regulation:

§47 Aufenthaltsräume

Aufenthaltsräume müssen eine lichte Raumhöhe von mindestens 2,40 m haben.

Aufenthaltsräume im Dachraum müssen eine lichte Raumhöhe von mindestens 2,20 m über mindestens der Hälfte ihrer Netto-Raumfläche haben; Raumteile mit einer lichten Raumhöhe bis zu 1,50 m bleiben außer Betracht.

Die Sätze 1 und 2 gelten nicht für Aufenthaltsräume in Wohngebäuden der Gebäudeklassen 1 und 2.
