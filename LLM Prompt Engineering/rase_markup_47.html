⚠ Note — Measurement Logic, not a RASE element: The 1.50 m clause defines the calculation perimeter for R2. When testing whether ≥ 2.20 m is achieved over "at least half" the net floor area, any portion of the room with a clear height ≤ 1.50 m is excluded from the total floor area count. The clause does not prohibit such areas from existing — it merely removes them from the arithmetic. A keyword-based system might tag this as an Exception; semantic analysis correctly identifies it as measurement methodology.
